The KS-LEAPES summer camp uses a unique distributed model - schools across the state of Kansas host local camps, offering a curriculum developed and led by K-State faculty. Camp activities include a mixture of hands-on, asynchronous activities, virtual tours of research and manfacturing facilties, and synchronous discussions with K-State faculty, industry leaders, and more!
